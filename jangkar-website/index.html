Welcome to The Jangkar

Jangkar means anchor — the place you stop, settle in, and end up staying a little longer.

Opened in August 2023, The Jangkar is a small, warm guesthouse on Tanah Barak Street in Canggu. Rooms face the garden and the anchor pool, evenings bring Bali's sunset sky, and great warungs and cafés are a two-minute stroll away.

Every room is air-conditioned, with a private bathroom, work desk, wardrobe and free Wi-Fi — simple, spotless and quietly stylish.

How do I book?

Choose your dates in the availability calendar above, or tap any WhatsApp button. We'll confirm availability and your rate in the chat — nothing to pay just to ask.

Can you arrange an airport pickup?

Yes. Ngurah Rai International Airport is about 16 km away. Airport transfers are available for an extra charge — just add it to your request.

Is breakfast available?

Yes, breakfast can be served in your room, and room service is available. Ask us about breakfast options when you book.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely — children of all ages are welcome and the pool is open to all ages. Family rooms are available; message us about extra-bed needs.

Are pets or parties allowed?

To keep things peaceful for every guest, pets are not allowed and we don't host parties, events or hen/stag groups.

Is there parking?

Yes, free private parking is available on site, no reservation needed. Bike rental can also be arranged.
